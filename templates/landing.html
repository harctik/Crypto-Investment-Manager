Know what your crypto portfolio could lose.

QuantRisk tracks your holdings, measures their risk, compares ways to split them and tests simple trading strategies on price history.

Open the app Run it locally

What it does

Portfolio
Record positions and trades, then see value, profit and loss, and how the portfolio is split. Export to CSV.
Prices and alerts
Market data from CoinGecko, a watchlist you can add coins to, and alerts when a price goes above or below your target.
Risk
Volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, value at risk, and RSI, MACD and Bollinger Band readings for each coin.
Allocation
Mixes for best Sharpe ratio, best return and lowest risk, and a risk-parity target compared with your current holdings.
Rules and stress tests
Check holdings against allocation limits, flag drift and correlated pairs, and see how market scenarios would move the total.
Backtests
A moving-average crossover on the dashboard. The command line also compares RSI, Bollinger Band and ensemble strategies with buy and hold.

How the numbers are calculated

Every result is an estimate built from past prices. It is not financial advice.

ResultMethod
Value at risk, expected shortfallHistorical, at 95% confidence
Optimised mixesMean-variance optimisation with SciPy’s SLSQP solver. Random sampling is used only if SciPy is missing.
Monte Carlo stress10,000 runs by default, up to 50,000. Each coin is sampled independently, so links between coins are not modelled.
Market scenariosSix fixed shocks, from a 60% fall to a 50% rally
Price estimatesRidge regression on price indicators with scikit-learn, otherwise linear regression
BacktestsFees and slippage are not modelled

Run it locally

A Flask app with a SQLite database. From the project folder, install, start, then open localhost:5000.

pip install -r requirements.txt
python app.py

There are no built-in passwords. Choose “Create an account” on the sign-in page.
